# Orrery Lab — Project Context

## 1. Project Overview

Orrery Lab is an independent, artist-led music and creative-technology studio. It creates emotionally driven work across music, interactive media, rhythm games, artistic research, and creative software.

The project is founded on a central idea:

> Separate worlds can become one coherent universe when they orbit the same emotional centre.

The name **Orrery** refers to a mechanical model of a planetary system. It represents a system in which distinct bodies retain their individual identities while moving as part of a larger structure. **Lab** represents experimentation, research, and continuous discovery.

Orrery Lab is the formal studio identity. **Orrery** is the public-facing world audiences enter.

## 2. Purpose

Orrery Lab exists to create meaningful experiences that remind people that beauty, curiosity, joy, and emotional depth can still be found in an imperfect world.

Its work should not exist only to satisfy algorithms, imitate trends, or generate attention. Reach and commercial sustainability matter, but they must support the creative purpose rather than replace it.

## 3. Positioning

Orrery Lab is positioned as:

- an independent music and creative-technology studio;
- a home for work that cannot be fully expressed through one genre or discipline;
- a bridge between emotional storytelling and technical experimentation;
- an artist-led system connecting music, tools, writing, and collaboration;
- a studio for distinctive projects requiring a strong artistic world.

Orrery Lab is **not** positioned as:

- a generic music-production service;
- a software company disconnected from artistic practice;
- a content factory;
- a conventional label built around commercial trends;
- a collection of unrelated experiments;
- a generic space-themed brand.

## 4. Initial Scope

### Primary activity

Music creation is the centre of Orrery Lab.

This includes:

- original releases;
- rhythm-game compositions;
- music for games and interactive media;
- commissioned compositions;
- arrangement and orchestration;
- electronic production;
- music-related sound design;
- selected collaborations and remixes.

### Secondary activity

Creative documentation developed from completed or active work:

- production notes;
- composition breakdowns;
- artistic essays;
- technical explanations;
- process reflections;
- case studies.

### Experimental activity

Creative technology emerging from real artistic needs:

- music utilities;
- MIDI tools;
- workflow scripts;
- browser-based musical tools;
- generative systems;
- plugins;
- interactive experiments;
- open-source projects.

### Future activities

The following should appear only after sufficient work, audience, and operational capacity exist:

- label activities;
- community programmes;
- workshops;
- educational resources;
- expanded artist support.

## 5. Brand Architecture

### Orrery Lab

The main studio, company, and creative umbrella.

### Keyaki

An artist identity associated with energetic, electronic, rhythm-game, and structurally intense work.

### Rei Tsukigaki

An artist or composer identity associated with cinematic, emotional, narrative, and orchestral work.

The identities should remain distinct while visibly connected to the same creative system.

Example relationships:

- Music by Keyaki
- Composed by Rei Tsukigaki
- Produced at Orrery Lab
- Released through Orrery
- Technology developed by Orrery Lab

## 6. Core Audiences

### Primary audiences

- rhythm-game communities;
- game developers;
- tournament and digital-event organisers;
- electronic-music listeners;
- game-music listeners;
- audiences interested in cinematic and emotionally intense work;
- creators seeking distinctive original music;
- independent artists seeking unusual collaborations.

### Secondary audiences

- music producers;
- composers;
- audio programmers;
- music-technology enthusiasts;
- creative coders;
- digital artists;
- animation and motion creators;
- researchers and writers interested in interdisciplinary practice.

## 7. Desired Audience Perception

A visitor should perceive Orrery Lab as:

- emotionally sincere;
- artistically ambitious;
- precise without being cold;
- experimental without being chaotic;
- professional without being corporate;
- mysterious without becoming inaccessible;
- technologically capable without being technology-led;
- coherent across different forms of work.

## 8. Project Decision Filter

A project belongs inside Orrery Lab when it has a clear reason to exist and satisfies several of the following conditions:

1. It expresses something meaningful.
2. It belongs to Orrery’s creative world.
3. It creates a relationship between ideas, disciplines, cultures, or people.
4. Its complexity serves expression.
5. It can be completed with the available capacity.
6. It contributes artistically, professionally, or financially.
7. It remains worth making even if its public numbers are modest.

## 9. Website Role

The website is not only a container for information. It is the first Orrery Lab work many visitors will experience.

It must therefore function as:

- a brand introduction;
- a music portfolio;
- a creative archive;
- a clear professional contact point;
- a bridge between artist identities;
- a platform for future tools and writing;
- an artistic expression of the Orrery system.

The website must communicate that distant ideas can enter the same orbit, technology can serve emotion, and complexity can become coherence.
